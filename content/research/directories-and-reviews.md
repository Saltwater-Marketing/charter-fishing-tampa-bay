Conventions used below:
- Text in quotation marks is copied character for character from the URL beside it, including the original typos and spacing.
- **UNCONFIRMED** means we could not show that the listing belongs to our captain.
- "Marketing copy" marks text from a site built by an agency (Saltwater Marketing, BookedCharter.Marketing). It sits on the captain's own site but may not be in his own words.
- In hand-copied quotes, curly apostrophes and quotation marks may have been typed as straight ones. The FG Charters widget reviews are pasted from the raw data exactly, curly apostrophes included.
- Google Maps dates are relative ("6 months ago") and are measured from 2026-11-09.

### Facts that contradict the current site

1. **Offshore guest limit.** The site says up to 4. Vally sells offshore for **1 to 6** (`maxTotalGroupSize: 6`; 5th guest +$75, 6th +$150). Rob's own offshore trips are also up to 6.
2. **Nearshore price.** The site says from $850. Vally shows **"From $650"** for a 4-hour nearshore trip. $850 is the 6-hour price.
3. **Grouper trip.** The site says $650. The "Inshore Grouper Trip" exists in Vally but is **inactive** and not shown on the booking page. Its season window is 1 to 14 September (Vally's months count from 0). Prices when active: 4h $650, 6h $800, 8h $1,000. Barotrauma and FG Charters both still sell a grouper trip.
4. **Guest limits by trip (the site says "unknown").** Vally: inshore 1–6, nearshore 1–6, offshore 1–6, shark 1–6, snook **1–3**, redfish **1–4** (4th guest +$75), tarpon **1–4** (3rd +$75, 4th +$150), crappie **1–4** (this matches the site).
5. **Crappie licence.** Vally crappie trip, "What to Bring": "FL freshwater fishing licenses." The customer supplies the licence; it is not included. Every saltwater trip says "We cover the fishing license for all participants."
6. **Tarpon season.** Vally limits tarpon to "May 1 - Jul 31". FG Charters' own tarpon trip runs May 1 to Aug 31.
7. **"[95+] years combined".** The stated figures add up to 40+ (Ashmore) + 25 (Pruner) + 30 (Gonzalez) + 30 (O'Neal) = **125+**, before counting Rob Hahn's "decades". 95+ only works if one 30-year captain is left out.
8. **"[850+] five star reviews".** The live site says "nearly 850+ 5-star online reviews" and "(over 800 5 star reviews)". The confirmed Google counts are 957 (Apollo Beach Fishing Charters, Gavin's own business) + 37 (FG Charters) + 10 (Barotrauma) = **1,004**, almost all on Gavin's profile. The brand itself has no Google profile and no reviews of its own. Any claim should say the reviews belong to the captains' own businesses.
9. **Gavin's own pricing differs from the brand's.** On his own site, prices cover **4 guests**, each extra guest is **$50**, and the 6-hour trip is **$750**. Vally charges +$75 and +$150 for the 5th and 6th guests. His own site also says 4-hour trips are **$650** (the brand's inshore 4h is $600). He can take "parties up to 25" on multiple boats.
10. **Frankie's capacity.** fgcharters.com/about says "a guest capacity of up to four anglers". Vally and Captain Experiences sell his trips for up to 6.
11. **Offshore distance wording.** Brand Vally offshore: "This trip starts where the nearshore trip stops! At the 9 mile state waters boundary." Rob's Barotrauma offshore: "7 to 30 miles out". Barotrauma nearshore: "3 to 7 miles off the coast". FG nearshore: "from the mouth of Tampa bay out to nine miles".
