## Fishing where the bay meets the Gulf

Being at the mouth of the bay means quick access to both: snook, redfish and trout on the inside, reef and bottom fish just outside.

One guest, Cody G., took his son on his first deep sea trip out of Anna Maria with Capt. Ashmore. In his words, "They patiently taught my son the ropes."
