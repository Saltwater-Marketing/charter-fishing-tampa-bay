## Fishing the east side of Tampa Bay

From Apollo Beach it's a short run to the flats, mangrove shorelines and channel edges that inshore trips work for redfish, snook and trout.

In winter, warm water around the Big Bend power station draws manatees in numbers, and you may see them on the way out. Sightings are never guaranteed.
