## Where were the crappie?

In the grass. The fish moved into the grass on Lake Kissimmee, and the best action came along the grass edges and openings where they were staging and feeding.

## How was the bite?

Consistent. Recent trips produced full limits, with anglers catching up to 25 fish per person. Once we found a school, the action stayed steady.

## What were they eating?

Light tackle with jigs worked very well, and minnows did too. It's simple, hands on fishing that keeps everyone on the boat busy.

## Who is this trip good for?

Families, beginners and anyone who wants fish for the table. Calm water and short travel make it one of our easiest trips. [See crappie trips](/crappie-fishing/)
