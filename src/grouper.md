## Gag season inside the bay

Gag grouper open for a short Gulf window each year. In 2026 it ran September 1 to 30, and that's when our inshore grouper trip goes on the booking calendar.

The 2026 season started strong. Two weeks in, our report from inside the bay said the gag bite "remains hot on live pinfish and grunts," steady and consistent. The [September 2026 roundup](/fishing-reports/september-2026-fishing-roundup-tampa-bay/) has the rest of that month, and the full veterans trip is in [Grouper, Tripletail and a Gift We Won't Forget](/fishing-reports/grouper-tripletail-wapiti-combat-veterans-september-2026/).

## Red grouper the rest of the year

Outside gag season, red grouper keep coming up. In January 2026 they were part of the bottom bite both nearshore and offshore, and in September they led the offshore bite alongside red snapper.

For red grouper, book a [nearshore trip](/nearshore/) inside nine miles or an [offshore trip](/offshore-fishing/) past it.

## Grouper questions

### When can we keep a gag?

Only during the Gulf gag season, which is set each year and is short. In 2026 it was September 1 to 30.

### Can we keep a red grouper?

When it's open and legal size, yes. Your captain follows the current Florida and federal rules on the day.

### Why can't I book a grouper trip right now?

The inshore grouper trip only opens on the booking calendar during gag season. The rest of the year, nearshore and offshore trips are the way to fish for grouper.
