## Grouper fishing follows the water temperature

In the cooler months, grouper move closer to Tampa Bay, so bay and nearshore trips get on them. In the warmer months they push farther offshore, and the runs get longer and the drops deeper.

We fish wrecks, reefs, ledges and other bottom structure, picked by the season, the depth and what's open to keep.

> "Been out multiple times and always have a cooler full of fish." Scott M.

## The grouper we target

### Gag grouper

The one we see most. Gags pull hard for the bottom and eat even better, but the keeping season is short, often only a few weeks. In 2026 it ran September 1 to 30. [CONFIRM 2027 DATES]

### Red grouper

More common when we can drop a little deeper, and a favorite when they're in season. On our September offshore trips, red grouper and red snapper led the bite whenever the weather let us out.

## What about goliath grouper?

We get asked all the time. Goliaths are protected, catch and release only, and landing one takes very heavy gear and more strength than most people expect. We don't run trips that target them. If one shows up on a ledge we're fishing, your captain will talk through it with you.

## Grouper fishing by season

### Fall

Gag season opens, and the bite can be steady from the first day.

### Winter and spring

Grouper hold closer to the bay, and nearshore bottom stays productive.

### Summer

Grouper move offshore. [See offshore trips](/offshore-fishing/)

## Grouper charter questions

### When can we keep gag grouper?

Only during the short Florida season, which changes each year.

### Can we fish for grouper out of season?

Yes, catch and release, and other bottom fish are usually biting too.

### Inshore, nearshore or offshore?

It depends on the time of year. Your captain will suggest the trip that puts you on fish. [Nearshore trips](/nearshore/)
