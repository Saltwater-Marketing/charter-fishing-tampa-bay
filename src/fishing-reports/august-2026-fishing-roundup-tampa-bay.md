Eleven oversized trout in a row. That's how one August morning went, before the twelfth fish finally fit the legal slot.

## Big trout in the troughs

The trout were working the troughs on the incoming tide, where moving water pushes bait through the deeper cuts. Once our anglers found them, the action didn't let up. Big female trout like these are a good sign for the bay, and they all went back.

## Redfish pushing in

All month, redfish numbers built as more fish moved in from the surrounding waters. Early in August they were sticking close to the shoreline. By the end of the month the trend was pointing up for the fall. [More on redfish trips](/redfish/)

## Sharks stayed busy

The smaller sharks were very active through August, giving guests plenty of shots and steady action. [More on shark trips](/shark/)

## Mixed bags with Capt. Pruner

Capt. Pruner spent a week fishing the bay the way it wanted to be fished, taking whatever was willing to bite. A solid mixed bag came aboard nearly every day, which made for relaxed trips that worked for families and first timers.
