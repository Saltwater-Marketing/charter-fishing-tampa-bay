If filling the cooler was the goal in January, bottom fishing was hard to beat. Every drop came with the chance of something different coming over the rail.

## Sheepshead everywhere

January was excellent for sheepshead. We found good numbers stacked on reefs, rock piles, docks and bridge pilings, and when they were there, the bites kept coming. They don't get the attention redfish or grouper do, but their clean, mild fillets make them one of the best eating fish in the bay.

## Nearshore bottom fishing

Closer to shore, our captains were catching plenty of white grunt, mangrove snapper, red grouper and lots of sheepshead. It was steady fishing and a good fit for groups with mixed experience. [See nearshore trips](/nearshore/)

## Offshore

Offshore trips produced bigger mixed bags on deeper structure. [See offshore trips](/offshore-fishing/)

## Crappie on Lake Kissimmee

Inland, the crappie moved into the grass on Lake Kissimmee and staged along the grass edges and openings. Recent trips produced full limits, up to 25 fish per person, on light tackle with jigs and minnows. Once we found a school, the action stayed steady. [See crappie trips](/crappie-fishing/)
