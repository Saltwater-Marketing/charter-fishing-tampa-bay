On Lake Kissimmee, January trips produced full limits of crappie, up to 25 fish per person. Back on Tampa Bay, bottom fishing was the way to fill a cooler.

Here's the month, water by water.

## Lake Kissimmee: crappie in the grass

The crappie moved into the grass on Lake Kissimmee. The best action came along the grass edges and openings, where the fish were staging and feeding.

Light tackle with jigs did most of the work, and minnows worked well too. Once a school was located, the bites stayed steady. See [crappie trips](/crappie-fishing/).

## Bridges and docks: sheepshead

January was excellent for sheepshead. Good numbers were stacked on reefs, rock piles, docks and bridge pilings.

Shrimp on jigs was the go to presentation. The bite is subtle, so it takes patience, but sheepshead fight hard all the way to the boat. They're also some of the best eating fish in the bay: clean, mild fillets.

## Nearshore: a mixed bottom bite

Closer to shore, trips were catching plenty of white grunt, mangrove snapper and red grouper, plus lots of sheepshead. It was steady fishing, a good fit for groups with mixed experience. See [nearshore trips](/nearshore/).

## Offshore: bigger mixed bags

Offshore trips produced the bigger bags. Red grouper, lane snapper, mangrove snapper, triggerfish, porgies and hogfish (hog snapper) all showed up over the rail. See [offshore trips](/offshore-fishing/).
