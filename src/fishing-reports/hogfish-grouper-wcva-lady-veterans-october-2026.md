Two boats, a full crew of lady veterans, and a little bit of everything coming up from the bottom. That was our second charter with the Wapiti Combat Veterans Foundation. It was a good day had by all.

## Back on the water with WCVA

We took WCVA out for a [grouper and tripletail trip in September](/fishing-reports/grouper-tripletail-wapiti-combat-veterans-september-2026/). This time it was the women veterans' turn, and we ran two boats so everyone could fish together.

## Grunts, grouper, triggerfish and hogfish

The bottom bite gave us a real mixed bag. Grunts, grouper, triggerfish and hogfish all came over the rail, along with a few other species.

Hogfish are always a good one to see. They're one of the best eating fish you can catch off Tampa Bay, and they don't show up on every trip.

Fall is a comfortable time to be bottom fishing out of Tampa Bay. The summer heat is gone, and a day like this one shows how many species can turn up on one trip. [More on grouper trips](/grouper/)

## The part you can't weigh

The fish were good, but they weren't what the captains talked about after the trip. In the captain's words: "The conversations, the camaraderie, and the smiles were a blessing to see."

## Thank you, ladies

Thank you to every veteran who came out, and thank you to WCVA for trusting us with another day on the water. We'd be glad to do it again.
