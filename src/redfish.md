## Redfish fishing in Tampa Bay, almost any day

Redfish live all over this bay: grass flats, mangrove shorelines, oyster bars, marsh creeks and river mouths. With that much water to work, there is usually somewhere they're feeding, in almost any weather and at any time of year.

> "He can put you on the fish." Jeff T.

## Where Tampa Bay redfish feed

### Mangrove shorelines

Reds slide up along the roots on the tide, looking for crabs and baitfish. It's where a lot of first redfish come from.

### Grass flats and oyster bars

When the water is clear and the fish are shallow, some days turn into sight fishing. Other days it's blind casting the edges. Your captain reads the day.

### When the bait moves

In October our captains were finding reds plentiful on the flats and along the mangrove tree lines, right where the fall mullet schools were moving.

## When redfish are best in Tampa Bay

### Late summer

More reds push into the bay from the surrounding waters, and the numbers build from there.

### Fall

The mullet run puts redfish on the feed alongside snook and sharks.

### The rest of the year

Reds are here year round. Winter and spring trips still find them on the flats.

## Redfish charter questions

### Can we keep a redfish?

When it's legal and in the slot, yes. Your captain follows current Florida rules.

### Is it good for first timers?

Yes. Reds hold in calm, shallow water, and your captain coaches every cast.

### Will we catch anything else?

Usually. Snook and trout live in the same water. [See inshore trips](/inshore/)
