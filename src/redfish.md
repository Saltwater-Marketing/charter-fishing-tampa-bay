## Reds through the year on Tampa Bay

Redfish are open all year in Tampa Bay, with an 18 to 27 inch slot. You can fish for them in any month; what changes is where they are and how many.

In August, our reports had them close to the shoreline and tucked into the mangroves. By mid September, redfish schools were pushing into the bay ahead of Red October.

In October they were plentiful on the flats and along the mangrove tree lines. The [October 2026 roundup](/fishing-reports/october-2026-fishing-roundup-tampa-bay/) has the details.

## Flats or mangroves

Some days the reds are up shallow on the flats, and you cast to fish you can see. Other days they're back under the bushes, and the cast has to land tight to the roots.

Your captain reads which it is that morning. A redfish trip is an [inshore trip](/inshore/) built around one fish, so snook and trout in the same water are a bonus.

## Redfish questions

### Can we keep a redfish?

Yes, when it measures inside the 18 to 27 inch slot. Your captain follows current Florida rules on bag limits.

### Is it a good first trip?

Yes. Reds feed in calm, shallow water, and your captain coaches the cast and the fight.

### Can we fish for snook too?

On the same water, often yes. If snook are the main goal, a [snook trip](/snook/) puts the day around them.
