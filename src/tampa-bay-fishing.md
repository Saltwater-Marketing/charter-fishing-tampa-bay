## One bay, a lot of different water

Tampa Bay is Florida's largest open water estuary. It runs from the rivers and backwaters around Tampa, through Old Tampa Bay and Hillsborough Bay, down to the lower bay and the Skyway at its mouth.

That range is why our team of captains can run so many different trips. Grass flats and mangroves on the edges, deeper channels and bridges through the middle, and the Gulf just past the Skyway.

## Which fishing charter fits which part of the bay

### The flats and backwaters

Inshore trips for redfish, snook and trout, on protected water with short runs. [Inshore fishing charters](/inshore/)

### Channels, bridges and structure

Sharks, grouper inside the bay, and tarpon in summer. [Shark](/shark/) · [Grouper](/grouper/) · [Tarpon](/tarpon/)

### Past the mouth of the bay

Nearshore and offshore trips to Gulf reefs, wrecks and ledges. [Nearshore](/nearshore/) · [Offshore](/offshore-fishing/)

## Fishing Tampa Bay through the year

Kings pass the mouth of the bay in spring and fall. Tarpon roll through from May to August. The fall mullet run puts snook, redfish and sharks on the feed together, and winter is sheepshead time on the docks and pilings. [Season guides](/fishing-guides/)
