## Ruskin and the south shore

Ruskin is home base for Capt. Pruner. His trips leave from 611 Destiny Drive, at Hooks Waterfront Bar and Grill, and he can pick guests up from hotels and docks in Ruskin.

From there he fishes the grass flats, mangroves and docks of the bay, from Bradenton Beach up into Tampa Bay. That's [inshore](/inshore/), [redfish](/redfish/) and [snook](/snook/) water.

Your captain confirms the meeting point when you book, because it can change with the wind and the trip.

## The bridges, passes and beaches

In summer, tarpon come through the bay, and our captains fish them on the bridges, around the passes and sometimes right on the beach. Snook use the same beaches and passes. See [tarpon trips](/tarpon/).

## From the mouth of the bay to nine miles out

Nearshore water starts at the mouth of Tampa Bay and runs out to about nine miles. Capt. Gonzalez fishes it for grouper and snapper, with kingfish, cobia and the occasional blackfin tuna or wahoo. See [nearshore trips](/nearshore/).

## Past the nine mile line

Nine miles out is where Florida's state waters end. Offshore trips start there, with a federal permit for reef and pelagic fish.

Capt. Hahn runs out of St. Petersburg and launches from "multiple ramps around Tampa Bay, based on weather and what's biting." See [offshore trips](/offshore-fishing/).

## North to the Nature Coast

Capt. Gonzalez has fished from Tampa Bay north into the Nature Coast for 30 years. His own trips launch out of Bayport, near Weeki Wachee and Pine Island. Read more about him on [Our Captains](/about/).

## Inland: the Kissimmee area lakes

When the crappie bite in winter, our captains trade the bay for Lake Kissimmee, Lake Toho and Crooked Lake, south of Orlando. See [crappie trips](/crappie-fishing/).
