## Fishing the lower bay and the Gulf from St. Pete

Inside, the lower bay has grass flats and mangrove edges for redfish, snook and trout. Past the beaches, nearshore trips reach reefs and hard bottom for snapper, grouper and sheepshead, and kings pass through in spring and fall.
