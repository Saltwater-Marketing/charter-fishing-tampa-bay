## Shark fishing close to home in Tampa Bay

Tampa Bay holds bull sharks, hammerheads, blacktips and nurse sharks. You don't need to run far. We fish flats, deeper channels and wrecks, depending on the season.

Shark tackle is heavy, and we bring all of it. Your job is to hold on.

## When shark fishing is best

### Late summer

In August our captains had the smaller sharks very active, with steady action for weeks and plenty of shots for everyone on board.

### Fall

Sharks shadow the mullet schools along the passes and channels, picking off bait at the edges.

## Who a shark trip is for

### Groups who want a fight

A shark on heavy tackle is a workout, and everyone gets a turn.

### Kids who've caught everything else

There's nothing like seeing one come up beside the boat.
