## Which sharks live in Tampa Bay

Capt. Gonzalez lists the local species he targets: blacktip, bonnethead, bull, tiger, lemon and hammerhead.

Our mid September 2026 report called shark fishing "pretty good all August," with the smaller sharks especially active, and the [September 2026 roundup](/fishing-reports/september-2026-fishing-roundup-tampa-bay/) had steady shark action on inshore trips.

## A trip for kids who want a story

## How long to book

Four hours is the fast trip with plenty of action. Six or eight hours gives your captain time to try more spots and baits when the sharks are slow, the way Darby's captain did on a cold February day.

Shark trips take up to six guests; the price covers four, with $75 for a fifth and $150 for a sixth.

## Shark questions

### Who runs shark trips?

Capt. Pruner, Capt. Gonzalez and Capt. Ashmore. Read about each on [Our Captains](/about/).

### Can we combine sharks with other fish?

Often, yes. Sharks share the bay with [redfish](/redfish/), snook and trout, so tell your captain what else you'd like to catch.

### Do we keep the sharks?

Talk to your captain when you book. Florida protects several of these species, including hammerheads and tiger sharks, so those go back after photos.
