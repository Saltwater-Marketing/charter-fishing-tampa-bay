## Inshore fishing on the flats and mangroves of Tampa Bay

Inshore trips stay inside the bay, on grass flats, mangrove shorelines, oyster bars and canals. The water is protected, so the ride stays smooth when the Gulf is choppy.

Most groups book six hours. That gives your captain time to fish the tide as it moves and to try a second spot if the first one is slow.

> "We were novice fishermen and he instructed us quite well." Eric M.

## What you'll catch on an inshore fishing charter

### Redfish

Reds feed all over the bay, in almost any weather. Late summer brings more of them in from the surrounding water, and by October our captains were finding them along the flats and the mangrove tree lines. [More on redfish trips](/redfish/)

### Snook

Snook hold tight to mangrove shorelines, docks, oyster bars and passes, and they hit hard and jump when they're hooked. Your captain fishes them with live pilchards, shrimp or artificial lures, depending on what they want that day. They're catch and release most of the year, with spring and fall windows to keep one. [More on snook trips](/snook/)

### Speckled trout

Trout sit in the troughs and wait for the tide to bring them a meal. The big ones are females, so most of the oversized fish go back to keep the bay producing.

### Whatever else is biting

In September our captains were also into schools of Spanish mackerel with jacks and ladyfish mixed in, plus mangrove snapper, white grunt and sharks, all on inshore trips. Tarpon move through the bay in summer.

## Inshore fishing in Tampa Bay by season

**Spring:** snook open for harvest and the trout are active on the flats.

**Summer:** tarpon roll through from May to August, and late summer brings more reds and steady sharks.

**Fall:** the mullet run, October into December, puts snook, redfish and sharks on the feed at the same time. [Read the mullet run guide](/fishing-guides/fall-mullet-run-tampa-bay/)

**Winter:** sheepshead stack up on docks, rock piles and bridge pilings.

## How your inshore fishing charter works

### Before you go

Reserve online with a 10% deposit. Your captain confirms where and when to meet. If the weather is unsafe, you get a new date or your deposit back.

### On the water

Your captain runs the boat, rigs the rods and moves you to where the fish are feeding. First timers get talked through every bite. As Jeff T. put it, "He can put you on the fish."

### After the trip

Keep what's legal and in season, and release the rest. [CONFIRM: the restaurant near the meeting point that cooks guests' catch]
