## Flats, mangroves, canals and the bay

Inshore trips stay inside Tampa Bay. That means grass flats, mangrove shorelines, marsh edges, canals and docks, where the water stays calm when the Gulf is rough.

Your captain picks the water by the tide and the wind on the day.

## What bites inshore

**Redfish** slide up the mangrove roots on the tide. In October our captains found them plentiful on the flats and along the mangrove tree lines. If reds are the whole point, book a [redfish trip](/redfish/).

**Snook** hold on docks, mangrove points and the beaches. They jump when hooked, and in season you can keep one inside the slot. More on our [snook trips](/snook/).

**Speckled trout** sit in the troughs and wait for the tide to bring them a meal. The big ones are females, so most oversized fish go back; the [August 2026 roundup](/fishing-reports/august-2026-fishing-roundup-tampa-bay/) tells the eleven trout story.

**The extras** make a slow tide a good one. In September the bay held schools of Spanish mackerel with jacks and ladyfish mixed in, plus mangrove snapper and white grunt. Read what turned up in our [September 2026 roundup](/fishing-reports/september-2026-fishing-roundup-tampa-bay/).

## Picking a trip length

Four hours fits young kids and a morning or afternoon on the water. Six hours is the length most groups book, because it leaves time to fish the tide as it moves and try a second spot.

Eight hours usually means fishing two tides. Morning trips leave at 8:00 AM, and 4 or 6 hour trips can also leave at 1:00 PM.

## Inshore questions

### Can kids come?

Yes. Inshore is the trip we suggest most for families: calm water, short runs and steady bites.

### Do we need fishing licenses?

No. Saltwater licenses are covered for everyone on board.

### Can we keep fish?

When a species is open and inside the slot, yes. Your captain follows Florida rules on the day and releases the rest.

### What if the weather turns?

A 10% deposit holds your date. If conditions are unsafe, you get a new date or your deposit back. Nearshore and [offshore trips](/offshore-fishing/) need calmer Gulf water than inshore does, so inshore is the usual backup on a windy day.
