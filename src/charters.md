## Fishing charters by the water you want

### [Inshore fishing charters](/inshore/), from $600

Redfish, snook and trout on the flats and mangroves inside the bay. Protected water and short runs.

### [Nearshore fishing charters](/nearshore/), from $850

A short run past the beaches to reefs and hard bottom for snapper, grouper, sheepshead and kings.

### [Offshore fishing charters](/offshore-fishing/), from $950

Bottom fishing on Gulf wrecks, reefs and ledges for grouper, snapper, cobia and more. Up to 4 guests.

### [Crappie fishing charters](/crappie-fishing/), from $550

Freshwater on Lake Toho, Lake Kissimmee and Crooked Lake near Orlando. Up to 4 guests.

## Every trip includes

Your captain, rods, reels, tackle, bait and fishing licenses, plus bottled water and ice. Reserve with a 10% deposit, refunded if weather cancels the trip.

Marinas around metro Tampa, depending on the captain and the trip. [DEPARTURE MARINAS] Crappie trips launch from whichever lake the fish are on that week.
